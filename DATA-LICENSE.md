# What licence covers what

Two different things live in this repository and they are not licensed the same
way. Read this before reusing anything.

## The software — O'Saasy

Everything in `app/`, `lib/`, `config/`, `test/` and the rest of the Rails
application is under the [O'Saasy License](LICENSE.md): do what you like with
it, including selling it, **except** run it as a hosted service that competes
with the original.

## The source data — CC0, and not ours to restrict

The files in `db/source` come from
[martj42/international_results](https://github.com/martj42/international_results)
under **CC0-1.0**. They are public domain. Nothing in this repository restricts
them, and nothing could: they were dedicated to the public domain by their
author and they stay that way.

## Facts are free. Everywhere. Always.

Match results, scores, dates, venues and goal totals are **facts**, and facts
carry no copyright — in the United States this was settled in *Feist
Publications v. Rural Telephone Service* (1991), which also rejected the idea
that effort alone earns protection. Anyone may take the numbers from this site
and use them for anything.

What *can* be protected in a compilation is the original selection,
coordination and arrangement — and any prose written around it. That is a
thinner protection than most people assume, and it is stated here plainly so
nobody has to guess.

## What is asked instead

Where this repository grows its own research — verified claims, citations,
source assessments — the request is attribution and a link, not a restriction.
If you build something with it, say where it came from.
